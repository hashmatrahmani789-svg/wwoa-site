Common questions about WWOA

Everything you need to know about our organization, our work, and how to get involved.

WWOA (Wahaj Welfare Organization for Afghanistan) is an independent, non-profit, non-political, and non-governmental organization founded in 2014. We work in education, healthcare, vocational training, and psychosocial support to help vulnerable communities across Afghanistan.

WWOA was originally founded in Jalalabad, Nangarhar Province, and later relocated its head office to Kabul to support a broader national presence.

Yes. WWOA is registered with Afghanistan's Ministry of Economy under registration number 3198.

WWOA has conducted a free medical camp treating 258 patients in Kama district, Nangarhar; a three-month vocational sewing training program for 10 women in Afshar; and three mental health and wellbeing sessions reaching 47 women. See our Projects page for full details.
