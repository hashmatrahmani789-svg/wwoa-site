Our Mission

A bridge between community needs and lasting opportunity.

WWOA exists to restore health, build skills, and renew hope for Afghanistan's women and children — through education, healthcare, and psychosocial support that meets people where they are.

What We Do

Where we focus our work

Health

Healthcare & Hygiene

Free medical camps, health education, and hygiene awareness for displaced and underserved communities.

Skills

Vocational Training

Practical skills training — like sewing and tailoring — that helps women earn income and support their families.

Wellbeing

Psychosocial Support

Mental health and wellbeing sessions that strengthen confidence, self-esteem, and healthy coping strategies.

Featured Project

Free Medical Camp — Mirzakhail, Kama District

In September 2014, WWOA partnered with the local public health department, district administration, and community elders to bring free medical care to a displaced settlement in Kama district, Nangarhar — home to 200–250 displaced people living among a host community of over 1,200.
