Where Your Support Goes

What a donation helps fund

Medical Camps

Medicine, staff, and logistics for free medical camps like the one held in Kama district, Nangarhar.

Vocational Training

Equipment and instruction for skills programs — like sewing and tailoring — that help women earn income.

Wellbeing Sessions

Facilitators and materials for mental health and confidence-building sessions for women.
