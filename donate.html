Active Campaign

Winter Campaign for Returnees from Pakistan and Iran

One hand alone may not lift a heavy load, but together our hands can change a family’s life.

This winter, WWOA supports Afghan families who have returned from Pakistan and Iran with almost nothing, especially families caring for members with disabilities. Many struggle to meet even their most basic needs, and your gift helps provide warmth, food, and essentials.

«وَتَعَاوَنُوا عَلَى الْبِرِّ وَالتَّقْوَىٰ»“Help one another in goodness and righteousness.” — Holy Quran, Al-Ma’idah 5:2

Every gift matters, however small. You don’t need to be wealthy to help; you just need to care. You can also volunteer your time.

Donations are collected and distributed with honesty, respect for each family’s dignity, and transparent reporting.

Where Your Support Goes

What a donation helps fund

Medical Camps

Medicine, staff, and logistics for free medical camps like the one held in Kama district, Nangarhar.

Vocational Training

Equipment and instruction for skills programs — like sewing and tailoring — that help women earn income.

Wellbeing Sessions

Facilitators and materials for mental health and confidence-building sessions for women.
